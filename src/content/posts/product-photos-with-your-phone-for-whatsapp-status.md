You do not need a studio or an expensive camera to make a product look good on WhatsApp Status. A mid-range phone, a window and a few habits are enough. Clear photos and short, well-framed videos make people stop scrolling, trust your product and ask for the price. This guide covers the basics for photos first, then short videos, then a quick editing routine.

## Why image quality matters for sales

On Status, a customer gives you about two seconds. If the photo is dark, blurry or cluttered, they move on. If it is bright and clear, they pause. Better images also reduce questions like "Is this the real colour?" and "What does the back look like?", which saves you time. ## Lighting: the biggest upgrade

### Use daylight

Place your product next to a window during the day, not in direct harsh sun. Soft, bright daylight makes colours look true and hides imperfections. Avoid mixing daylight with yellow room bulbs because colours will look off.

### Skip the flash

Phone flash creates harsh glare and flat, shiny surfaces. If you must shoot indoors at night, use two bright white lamps from both sides, or a ring light, and keep them slightly above the product.

### Fix shiny products

Glass, jewellery and plastic reflect light. Turn the product slightly, or place a sheet of white paper to soften the reflection.

## Backgrounds that sell

- Use a plain surface such as a white table, a clean cloth, a wooden board or a sheet of coloured card.
- Remove clutter from the frame. Move the cables, bags and other items out of view.
- Choose a background that contrasts with the product. A black phone case on white paper stands out. A white one on white does not.
- Keep the same background for a series so your Status looks consistent.

## Composition and framing

1. **Clean the lens.** A smudged lens is the most common reason for hazy photos. Wipe it with a soft cloth.
2. **Fill the frame.** Move closer or crop so the product takes up most of the picture, but leave a little space around it.
3. **Hold steady.** Rest your elbows on a table or use a small tripod. Tap the screen to focus on the product.
4. **Shoot three angles.** Front, side and a close-up of a detail such as texture, stitching or a label.
5. **Use the grid.** Turn on gridlines in camera settings to keep horizons and edges straight.
6. **Show scale.** Place a hand or a familiar object next to small items so people understand the size.

For clothing, hang items on a hanger against a wall or have someone model them. For food, shoot from above or at a slight angle in bright light and keep the plate clean.

## Short videos that people finish

Video gets more attention on Status when it is short and clear.

- **Length.** Aim for 10 to 20 seconds. WhatsApp shows long videos in segments, and most people will not watch to the end.
- **Hook in three seconds.** Show the result first, such as the finished cake or the clean sofa, then show how it is made.
- **One idea per clip.** Product demo, unboxing, before and after, or a quick tip.
- **Shoot vertically.** Status is a vertical format. Hold the phone upright.
- **Sound.** Speak clearly in a quiet place, or add simple captions because many people watch without sound.
- **Keep file size reasonable.** Long, high-resolution clips can fail on weak networks. Trim and avoid extra-large files.

## A simple editing routine (two minutes per photo)

You can edit inside your phone's gallery or a free app. Do only light edits.

1. **Crop** to remove extra background.
2. **Brightness and contrast.** Raise brightness slightly and add a little contrast.
3. **Colour accuracy.** Do not boost saturation too much. If the photo looks different from the real item, customers will complain.
4. **Sharpen slightly** if the image looks soft.
5. **Add a small logo or contact line** in a corner if you like, but keep it subtle.

Never use someone else's photos for your products. Customers notice, and it can cause copyright complaints.

## Build a repeatable shooting session

Pick one hour a week. Set up near a window, prepare your background, shoot your top products from three angles, record two or three short clips and edit them in a batch. ## Photo ideas for common product types

Different products need slightly different handling. These quick notes help you adjust.

- **Shoes and bags.** Shoot on a clean floor or table, include the sole or inside lining and one photo with the item in use. Stuff bags with paper so they hold their shape.
- **Phones and gadgets.** Wipe the screen and body, turn the screen on and show ports, accessories and what comes in the box.
- **Skincare and cosmetics.** Show the label clearly, the texture in a small swatch and the full set together.
- **Food.** Shoot near the window while the food is fresh, wipe the plate edges and show a portion that matches what the customer will receive.
- **Fabric and clothing.** Photograph in daylight so the colour is true, and show the pattern up close and the full length of the item.

## Keep your phone and files in good shape

A few small habits protect your work. Free up storage before a shooting session so the camera does not stop halfway. Keep a labelled folder for each product so you can find photos quickly when a customer asks for another angle. Delete near-duplicate shots at the end of the session, since a folder of fifty similar images wastes time. If you share an image with a customer, send the original instead of a screenshot, because screenshots lose quality. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a tripod?

No, but a cheap one makes your videos steadier and frees your hands. Before you buy one, try propping the phone against a stack of books.

### Should I use portrait mode?

Portrait mode can blur the background nicely on some phones, but it sometimes cuts around the edges of products badly. Check the result before you post and switch it off if edges look odd.

### How do I keep the colours accurate?

Shoot in daylight, avoid heavy filters and compare the photo with the real item on your screen. If they look different, adjust before posting, since customers who feel misled lose trust. See [building customer trust online](/blog/build-customer-trust-online-nigeria).

### Can I reuse photos in my catalogue?

Yes. The same clear photos work on Status and in your [WhatsApp Business catalogue](/blog/whatsapp-business-catalog-guide). Shoot once, use everywhere.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Heavy filters that change the colour of the product.
- Photos taken with a zoom that makes them grainy. Move closer instead.
- Screenshots of other sellers' ads.
- Very long videos recorded with the phone held sideways.
- Posting dark photos at night when people cannot see the detail.

## Check what works

After posting, look at your viewer counts and the replies you get. If photos with a certain background or angle bring more messages, repeat that style. [Checking who viewed your Status](/blog/how-to-check-who-viewed-whatsapp-status) takes a minute and shows you how people respond.
